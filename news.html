AI Is a Tool. Is It “Work” or “Gym”?

Many students use AI today. It can write, translate, and answer questions in a few seconds. But should you always use it?

Stuck on a word? Tap a word with a dotted line. You will see the Japanese.

Why AI is good 👍

  • AI is fast. It can explain a hard word in easy English.
  • AI does not get tired. You can ask the same question many times.
  • AI can be your tutor. It can check your English and give you hints.

Why AI can be bad 👎

  • AI makes mistakes. Sometimes it says wrong things, but it sounds very sure.
  • AI does not know your life. It does not know your school or your town.
  • If AI does everything for you, you do not learn. Your brain does not get stronger.

The big idea: AI is a tool 🛠️

A tool is good when you use it in the right place. Think about two places: work and the gym.

📦 At workYou must move many heavy boxes across the room. Nobody cares how you do it. So use a cart or a robot. That is smart!
🏋️ At the gymYou lift heavy weights to get strong. If a robot lifts the weights for you, you do not get stronger. The lifting is the point!

Some jobs are like work. You only need the result. Other jobs are like the gym. How you do it is important, because you learn from it.

Your English homework is like the gym. If AI writes it, your English does not get stronger.

Work jobs and gym jobs ✅

Work jobs: AI can help • Find the meaning of a word
• Make a quick list of ideas
• Translate a sign or a menu
Gym jobs: do it yourself • Write your own English sentences
• Practice speaking English
• Think of your own ideas

How should we use AI? 3 rules

  1. Ask for hints, not answers.
  2. Check important facts.
  3. Use AI for work jobs. Do gym jobs with your own brain.
AI is a tool. You are the boss. 💪

Talk with your partner 💬

  2. What is one gym job you want to do yourself?
